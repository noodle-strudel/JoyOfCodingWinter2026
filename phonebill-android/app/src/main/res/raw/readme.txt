[ Description ]
This app keeps track of phone bills from multiple customers.
The appcan save phone bills and load them when the app restarts.
The app allows for searching for specific phone calls between
two time periods or displaying all phone calls associated with
a customer.

All time fields are in the format mm/dd/yyyy hh:mm

[ Searching For Phone Calls ]
Not entering anything into the customer name displays all phone bills.
You can enter the customer name and check to search between
a time range.

[ Quick Access to Phone Bills ]
Tap on the phone bills in the main menu or the ones listed in the
search result to look at their associated phone calls.
